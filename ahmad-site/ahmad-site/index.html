How I work

Method, by phase

The same four phases on a mortgage product in 2012 and a travel tool in 2026. What changed is the toolkit, not the judgement.

Phase 01

Project management

  • Scrum, Kanban, roadmapping by phase
  • Milestone and release planning
  • Backlog prioritisation, estimation

I hand over: product strategy, roadmap, release plan, business case, budget plan.

Phase 02

Problem discovery

  • Jobs to be Done and user interviews
  • Usability tests, surveys, web analytics
  • PESTEL, Porter’s Five Forces, SWOT

I hand over: journey map, research report, personas, segmentation, competitor benchmarks.

Phase 03

Solution discovery

  • Design Thinking, Crazy 8s, wireframing
  • Prototype and think-aloud testing
  • A/B testing

I hand over: user stories to INVEST, story map, MVP canvas, validation report.

Phase 04

Implementation

  • No-code and low-code build
  • Work with engineering and design
  • Instrumentation and telemetry

I hand over: a working MVP, test results, launch plan, operating procedures, training.

About

Why a banker is applying for product roles

I spent eighteen years in the UAE deciding what financial products should exist, what they should cost, and whether they were working. Mortgages through a market crash. Personal finance. Business banking. Then the entire retail portfolio of the national postal operator, with the profit and loss attached to it.

What that teaches is not banking. It is how to get something built when risk, legal, operations and a board all hold a veto — and how to tell adoption from activity once it ships. Those are the two hardest parts of product management, and neither belongs to an industry.

I moved to Berlin in 2024, reached certified German B1, and retrained deliberately rather than repeating the same job in a new market. The programme work above is where the current toolkit was rebuilt: research, build, instrument, read the numbers, iterate — including the part where the numbers went wrong first.

I am looking for senior product roles in Berlin or remote, where the constraints are real and the results are measured.
